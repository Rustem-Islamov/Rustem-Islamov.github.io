About me

I am a final-year PhD student at University of Basel working under the supervision of Aurelien Lucchi. Prior to that, I obtained my Master diploma from Institut Polytechnique de Paris in Data Science and Bachelor diploma from Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology in Applied Mathematics. I am interested in Optimization and its applications to Machine Learning.
