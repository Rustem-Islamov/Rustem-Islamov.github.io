About me

I am a final-year PhD student at the University of Basel, advised by Aurelien Lucchi. I work at the intersection of the theory and practice of optimization for machine learning. My research focuses on improving our understanding of deep learning optimizers and training techniques, and on making them more efficient and scalable from first principles.

Outside of research, I play tennis and enjoy watching football and judo.
